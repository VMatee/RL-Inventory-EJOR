# Research agent rules

Follow docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md and the latest research brief. Never invent results, citations, proofs, data access, or successful reproductions. Label conjectures as conjectures. Never claim retail sales are uncensored latent demand. Maintain information-budget parity, held-out evaluation, and traceability of costs, seeds and hyperparameters. Do not change research scope without documenting the decision in docs/DECISION_LOG.md.
